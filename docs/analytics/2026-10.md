**Indexing moved within hours.** `/blog`, `/blog/webflow-multilingual-site-what-it-takes`

(crawled 27 Oct). Still out:

- `/work/seilas-ship-supplies`: **crawled, not indexed**, last crawl 10 Jul. It
  had 7 impressions in the 90-day window, so it probably dropped out. New.
- `/es/services`: crawled, not indexed. New.

**Spanish pages declare the English URL as canonical.** Every `/es/*` page ships
`<link rel="canonical">` pointing at its English twin (`/es` → `/`,
`/es/about` → `/about`, …) while hreflang says the two are alternates. The
`alternates.canonical` values in `src/app/[locale]/**/page.tsx` and
`layout.tsx:67` are hardcoded English paths. Google is currently overriding it
(URL inspection: Google-chosen canonical is the `/es` URL for `/es`,
`/es/about` and the ES ecommerce post), so the damage is limited, but it is a
contradicting signal; `/es/services` being excluded is consistent with it, not
proven to be caused by it. Also: no `x-default` hreflang, and the sitemap lists
`/es/`, which 308s to `/es`.

**Where the impressions come from.** Spain is 260 of 581 (45%), nearly all from
`/es/blog/webflow-ecommerce-made-to-order` at position ~31 with no clicks. USA is
157 at position 5.3, almost all brand and case-study names. The 18.1 average
position is that mix, not a ranking problem on the pages that matter.

**Weekly trend (GSC).** Impressions peaked the weeks of 10 and 17 Aug (86, 83)
and sit at 30–40 a week since mid-September. Clicks: 0–2 a week, ~2 a month,
flat for the whole period.

**PageSpeed (lab, mobile / desktop perf):** `/` 96 / 95 · `/work` 98 / 100 ·
`/services` 99 / 100. Accessibility 96–100, best practices and SEO 100. Mobile
LCP 1.7–2.3 s. Still no field data.
